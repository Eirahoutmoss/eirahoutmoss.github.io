Aethelgard

A biosphere drifting in a cold, dense, still band of a giant planet's atmosphere: pressure is high, temperature low, chemical reactions very slow. Time seems to pass slowly because biological processes are sluggish; enigmatic fauna surviving since the universe's early ages, communicating by very low-frequency (infrasonic) vibrations and living for centuries, live here.
